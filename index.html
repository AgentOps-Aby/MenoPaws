You're probably here because…

  • Waking at 3am drenched in sweat
  • Hot flashes out of nowhere
  • Losing words mid-sentence
  • Unpredictable periods
  • Mood swings or sudden rage
  • Anxiety you can't explain
  • Exhausted no matter how you rest
  • Achy joints and a changing body
  • Painful sex or low desire
  • Being told it's "just stress"
  • Not knowing if it's perimenopause
  • Feeling alone in all of it

You're not imagining it. These are common signs of perimenopause and menopause.

Why this exists Most of what we know about menopause comes from company-owned apps, one brand of smartwatch, or clinic patients, and women get little back for sharing. MenoPaws captures your story in your own words, gives you something useful right away, and with your permission adds it to an open dataset anyone can study.
